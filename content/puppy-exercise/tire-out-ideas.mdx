  Combine moderate physical activity with genuine mental engagement — several options work well indoors or in bad weather.
</AnswerFirstSummary>

## Outdoor, Physical

Sniffing walks; free play in a safe enclosed space; structured fetch/tug in moderation.

## Indoor and Weather-Independent

Short training sessions; food-based enrichment; indoor hallway fetch; hide-and-seek with treats/toys; short obstacle-style games (avoid repetitive jumping/stairs for young/growing puppies).

## Rainy Day / Winter Adaptations

Shift balance toward mental engagement and short weather-appropriate breaks; indoor training and food enrichment can substitute for a longer walk without a welfare cost.

## Quick Reference

| Situation | Good options |
|---|---|
| Normal day, good weather | Sniffing walk, free play, short training |
| Bad weather | Indoor training, food enrichment, short breaks |
| Very young/growing puppy | Favor moderate/self-directed over repetitive high-impact indoor games |
| Limited time | Short training sessions are genuinely tiring |

## Common Mistakes

- Relying only on physical activity.
- Using repetitive high-impact indoor games as a substitute.
- Eliminating all outdoor time in bad weather.
