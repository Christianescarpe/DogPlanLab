## The Popular Claim, Fact-Checked

This research pass traced the ratio claim through multiple sources and found no genuine peer-reviewed study establishing it — the citation trail led to blog posts citing each other, not research. Not repeated here.

## What Is Genuinely Supported

Scent-based and sniffing activities are a real, distinct area of scientific interest with published research on welfare/enrichment value.<sup>1</sup> Sniffing engages different systems than physical exertion — genuine mental engagement, not "exercise-lite." A precise numeric trade-off is not supported.

## The Practical Takeaway

Treat physical exercise and mental stimulation as two genuinely different needs. A puppy with plenty of physical activity but still restless may specifically be missing mental engagement, not more exercise — and vice versa.
