## What Actually Matters First

1. **Safety and supervision.** Puppy-proof one main area, and don't leave your puppy unsupervised and unconfined until house training is well underway. This isn't optional groundwork — it's what everything else depends on.
2. **A loose daily rhythm.** Wake, potty, eat, activity, nap, repeat. It doesn't need to be precise yet — see the [Daily Puppy Schedule](/puppy-schedule/) for a full breakdown.
3. **House training, starting immediately.** Not "once things settle down" — from day one. See [Potty Training](/potty-training/) for the full method.
4. **Calm first nights.** The first few nights are often the hardest part, and that's normal, not a sign something's wrong. See [First Week With a New Puppy](/new-puppy/first-week/) for a day-by-day plan through the first seven days specifically.

Everything below builds on top of these four — training commands, deeper socialization, and refining routines all go more smoothly once safety, rhythm, house training, and reasonable nights are in place.

## A Note on Pace

New owners often feel behind because there's a long list of "things you're supposed to do" with a new puppy. Most of that list can wait. The four priorities above are what actually need attention in the first days — the rest (structured training, broader socialization, refining schedules) has more room than it feels like it does.
