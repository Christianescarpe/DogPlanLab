## The Core Method

Work with your veterinarian or your own judgment to gradually expose your puppy to the variety of people they'll encounter in life, rewarding calm or curious engagement with praise, play, or treats — and allowing your puppy to withdraw if they seem uncomfortable rather than forcing continued contact.<sup>1</sup>

## Variety Matters

Different ages, heights, appearances (hats, beards, glasses, uniforms), and mobility (wheelchairs, walkers, strollers) are all worth including — not because your puppy needs to meet a checklist of every possible person, but because genuine variety builds broader comfort than repeatedly meeting similar-looking people.

## Pacing

Start at a comfortable distance. If your puppy looks calm and curious, let them approach and investigate. If they seem uncertain, don't push closer — hold distance, reward calm behavior, and let confidence build gradually rather than forcing contact.

## What to Do Today

1. Choose one new type of person to introduce this week — not five at once.
2. Start at a distance your puppy is comfortable at.
3. Pair the person's presence with something good — a treat, calm praise, play.
4. Let your puppy choose to approach; don't carry or push them toward a person.
5. End the interaction while it's still positive, not once your puppy seems bored or done.

If your puppy seems reluctant with a specific type of person repeatedly, increase distance and slow down rather than repeating the same close-up introduction hoping it improves on its own.

## Common Mistakes

- Letting strangers rush up to pet your puppy without warning.
- Forcing physical contact — picking your puppy up and putting them in a stranger's arms.
- Assuming more exposure is automatically better regardless of your puppy's comfort.

## When Professional Help May Be Appropriate

If your puppy consistently shows fear (not just mild hesitation) around people despite a slow, positive approach, see [Puppy Fear: Scared of People](/puppy-fear/scared-of-people/) for the recovery-focused guidance that page is built for.
