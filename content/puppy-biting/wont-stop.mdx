<AnswerFirstSummary>
  Usually inconsistency, a toy less rewarding than continuing to bite, unaddressed overstimulation before you start, or simply needing more repetitions — genuine "nothing works" cases are less common than one of these gaps.
</AnswerFirstSummary>

## Check Consistency First

Redirection only works if it's the same, every time, from everyone in the household.

## Check the Reward Math

A toy less exciting than your hand/moving body loses every time — try something that moves, squeaks, or can be tugged.

## Check Timing

Redirecting at peak arousal is much harder than catching the buildup earlier.

## Check Whether You're Accidentally Reinforcing It

Fast movement, loud reactions, or chasing can look like continued play from your puppy's view.

## If You've Checked All of This

1. Give it more time — new patterns take more repetitions than expected.
2. Make sure your puppy's getting enough outlets outside these interactions.
3. Consider whether body language has shifted (stiff/intense vs. loose/bouncy) — if so, see [Is My Puppy Being Aggressive, or Just Playing?](/puppy-biting/aggression-or-play/)
4. A certified trainer observing in person is a reasonable next step if nothing above identifies a gap.

## Troubleshooting

| What to check | Common finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Everyone responding the same way? | Inconsistency | Align the household |
| Is the toy actually appealing? | Less rewarding than biting | Higher-value toy |
| Redirect timing | Too late in escalation | Interrupt earlier |
| Your reaction fast/loud/chase-like? | Accidentally reinforcing | Go genuinely still and calm |
| Body language changed? | Possibly not typical play anymore | See [Aggression or Play](/puppy-biting/aggression-or-play/) |

## Common Mistakes

- Assuming "nothing works" without checking the gaps above.
- Giving up on consistency quickly.
- Escalating to punishment.
- Ignoring a genuine change in character, not just frequency.

## Safety and Behavior Red Flags

Shift from loose/bouncy to stiff/intense biting; increasing force or frequency despite consistent effort over a couple of weeks.
