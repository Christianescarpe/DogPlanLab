  "Accidents" isn't one problem — it's several different patterns with different causes and fixes. This page helps you identify which pattern matches your puppy.
</AnswerFirstSummary>

## Start With the Pattern, Not the Symptom

Insufficient opportunity, timing gaps, incomplete learning, environmental distraction, too much freedom too soon, an unclear potty location, substrate preference, incomplete cleanup, and (less often) a medical cause can all produce the same surface symptom.

## Diagnostic Table

| What you're seeing | Likely pattern | Go here |
|---|---|---|
| Goes outside, then has an accident shortly after coming back in | Trip cut short, distraction, or excitement urination | [Puppy Pees Inside After Going Outside](/potty-training/pees-inside-after-going-outside/) |
| Won't eliminate outside at all, then goes inside | Distraction, unfamiliarity, surface preference, or fear | [Puppy Won't Pee Outside](/potty-training/wont-pee-outside/) / [Puppy Won't Poop Outside](/potty-training/wont-poop-outside/) |
| Was reliable, now regularly having accidents again | Regression | [Potty Training Regression](/potty-training/regression/) |
| Accidents specifically in the crate | Duration, setup, or incomplete training | [Crate Accidents](/potty-training/crate-accidents/) |
| Accidents specifically overnight | Distinct nighttime causes | [Nighttime Accidents](/potty-training/nighttime-accidents/) |
| Urinates during greetings/excitement, seemingly involuntary | Excitement/submissive urination | [Excitement & Submissive Urination](/potty-training/excitement-submissive-urination/) |
| Accidents specifically when you're not home | Confinement duration or alone-time-specific issue | [Accidents When Left Alone](/potty-training/accidents-when-alone/) |
| Urinating much more often than usual, in general | Possible medical cause | [Frequent Urination](/potty-training/frequent-urination/) |
| Won't use the same spot twice / keeps changing locations | Substrate/location inconsistency | See "Inconsistent Location" below |

## Inconsistent Potty Location

Commit to one specific spot for every outdoor trip for at least a couple of weeks, resisting the urge to let your puppy "choose" a new area, before reassessing.

## A General Principle That Applies Regardless of Pattern

Never punish an accident you find after the fact — your puppy can't connect a correction to the act, and punishment tends to teach puppies to hide when eliminating.

## What to Do Today

Use the diagnostic table above. If more than one pattern seems to apply, address the most frequent one first.

## Common Mistakes

- Assuming all accidents share one cause.
- Assuming stubbornness, spite, or dominance are driving accidents.
- Skipping straight to a "fix" without identifying the pattern first.

## Medical Red Flags

Straining, very frequent small-amount urination, blood in urine, repeated diarrhea, vomiting, or lethargy — see [Frequent Urination](/potty-training/frequent-urination/) and talk to your veterinarian.
