<AnswerFirstSummary>
  Alone-time-specific accidents usually point to a missed potty opportunity, a confinement duration exceeding capacity, or (less often) genuine distress — these call for different responses.
</AnswerFirstSummary>

## Start With the Simplest Explanation

Did your puppy get a genuine potty opportunity right before you left? A missed or rushed pre-departure trip is one of the most common, fixable causes.

## The Three Most Likely Patterns

- **Missed opportunity** — no trip immediately before departure, or a rushed one.
- **Duration exceeds capacity** — a math problem, not a behavior problem; build alone-time gradually (see [Puppy Home Alone](/puppy-home-alone/)).
- **Distress specifically tied to your absence** — less common; if paired with destructive behavior, excessive vocalization, or escape attempts specifically when you're gone, that's worth flagging to your vet rather than self-diagnosing.

## What to Do

1. Add a deliberate, unhurried potty trip immediately before every departure.
2. Compare departure duration to demonstrated capacity; build up gradually if there's a mismatch.
3. If accidents persist despite a solid routine and reasonable duration, look for other distress signs before assuming it's purely potty-related.

## Troubleshooting

| Pattern | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| No accidents on days with a solid pre-departure trip | Missed opportunity was the cause | Keep the routine consistent |
| Accidents correlate with longer absences | Duration exceeds capacity | Build alone-time gradually — see [Puppy Home Alone](/puppy-home-alone/) |
| Paired with destructive behavior, vocalization, or escape attempts specifically during absence | Possible distress tied to being alone | See [Puppy Home Alone](/puppy-home-alone/)'s distress section; mention to your vet |

## Common Mistakes

- Assuming separation anxiety from an accident alone.
- Skipping the pre-departure trip.
- Extending duration faster than demonstrated capacity.

## Medical Red Flags

Frequent urination generally, straining, blood in urine, or lethargy — see [Frequent Urination](/potty-training/frequent-urination/) regardless of timing.
