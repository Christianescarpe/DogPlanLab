<AnswerFirstSummary>
  Independent play is a learnable skill — build it by rewarding calm, self-directed engagement with a toy while you're nearby but not actively participating, starting short and building up.
</AnswerFirstSummary>

## Why This Is a Skill, Not Just Temperament

A puppy who's never had the chance to self-occupy hasn't necessarily learned it's a safe, rewarding option — buildable like the related but distinct alone-time skill (see existing [Puppy Home Alone](/puppy-home-alone/)).

## How to Build It

1. Engaging item while you're in the room, doing something else.
2. Don't immediately engage every check-in.
3. Reward calm, self-directed engagement specifically.
4. Gradually extend independent stretches.

## Distinguishing This From Alone-Time Training

This is self-occupation while you're present; full alone-time comfort is a related, distinct skill covered at existing [Puppy Home Alone](/puppy-home-alone/).

## Quick Reference

| Stage | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Engaging item, you're present, not participating |
| 2 | Don't respond to every check-in |

## Common Mistakes

- Always immediately engaging on request.
- Expecting long stretches too soon.
- Conflating with full alone-time training.
