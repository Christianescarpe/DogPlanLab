<AnswerFirstSummary>
  Boredom commonly shows as restlessness, seeking constant attention, destructive behavior, or excessive digging — the fix is usually genuine mental engagement, though it's worth distinguishing boredom from other causes first.
</AnswerFirstSummary>

## Common Signs

Restlessness despite activity; seeking constant attention; destructive behavior when unsupervised (see existing [Puppy Chewing Everything](/puppy-chewing/everything/)); excessive digging with no other explanation.

## Boredom Isn't the Only Explanation

Rule out insufficient physical activity, overtiredness (see [Overtired Puppy](/puppy-sleep/overtired/)), or a medical cause before assuming boredom.

## What Actually Helps

Regular mental engagement (see [Puppy Enrichment](/puppy-enrichment/)); toy rotation; scent-based activity; independent-engagement skills (see [Teaching Independent Play](/puppy-enrichment/independent-play/)).

## On Digging Specifically

Excessive digging with no clear trigger (not prey scent, heat-seeking, or anxiety) is often boredom-related and responds to this page's general approach; a full dedicated digging treatment is outside this batch's scope.

## Quick Reference

| Sign | Check first | If boredom-driven, try |
|---|---|---|
| Restless despite exercise | Overtiredness | Add mental engagement |
| Destructive when unsupervised | See [Puppy Chewing Everything](/puppy-chewing/everything/) | Enrichment + confinement |
| Constant attention-seeking | See existing barking/attention content | Independent-play skill building |
| Excessive digging, no clear trigger | Rule out specific cause | General enrichment approach |

## Common Mistakes

- Assuming boredom without checking other causes.
- Adding exercise when the gap is mental.
- Punishing boredom-driven behavior instead of addressing the gap.

## Safety and Behavior Red Flags

Sudden-onset restlessness/digging with no explanation, especially alongside other changes.
