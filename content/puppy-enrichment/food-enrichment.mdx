## Why Food-Based Enrichment Works

Turning food into a problem-solving/foraging activity engages natural behaviors a bowl doesn't — a genuine, widely accepted environmental-enrichment principle.

## The Main Categories

**Puzzle feeders** — manipulation-based food dispensing; introduce gradually. **Snuffle mats** — scent-based foraging specifically. **Lick mats** — repetitive licking, often useful as a calming tool. **Stuffed/food-dispensing toys** — often used during crate/alone-time (see existing [Crate Training](/crate-training/), [Puppy Home Alone](/puppy-home-alone/)).

## Practical Safety Notes

Portion awareness (this food counts toward daily intake, not extra); supervise new items until you know your puppy won't ingest non-food parts; appropriate difficulty (too hard too fast creates frustration); appropriate foods only, reasonable amounts on lick mats.

## Distinguishing Real Value From Marketing

The general welfare principle is well-supported; individual product superiority claims are commercial claims, not independently verified research.

## Common Mistakes

- Treating enrichment food as extra calories.
- Leaving a new toy unsupervised.
- Too-difficult a puzzle too soon.

## Safety and Behavior Red Flags

Attempting to chew apart/ingest toy pieces; persistent GI upset after a new enrichment food.
