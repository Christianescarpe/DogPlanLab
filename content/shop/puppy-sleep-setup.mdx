<AnswerFirstSummary>
  A durable, chew-resistant, machine-washable mat or bed suited to your puppy's current size — not oversized — is the main consideration for crate or bed bedding. White noise or a fan is a low-risk, genuinely reasonable addition for masking disruptive household or outdoor sounds, though it isn't a substitute for the routine-and-consistency approach covered in existing sleep content.
</AnswerFirstSummary>

## How to Choose

This is for anyone setting up a crate (see existing [Crate Training](/crate-training/)) or deciding [Where Should a Puppy Sleep](/puppy-sleep/where-should-puppy-sleep/).

## What to Compare

Chew-resistance — a very young or teething puppy will test bedding, see existing [Puppy Teething](/puppy-teething/); washability; appropriate size for your current crate or bed dimensions, not oversized; non-toxic materials; for white-noise devices, volume range and timer or auto-off options.

## Comparison

| Item | Best for | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Basic washable crate mat | Most puppies, early crate training | Chew-resistance for teething-stage puppies |
| Raised or orthopedic bed | Older puppies, larger breeds | Oversizing relative to current crate size |
| White noise machine or fan | Masking disruptive household or outdoor sound | Not a substitute for a consistent routine — see existing [Evening Routine](/puppy-schedule/evening-routine/) |

## Best For / Not Ideal For

A plush, easily-shredded bed is not ideal for a heavy teething chewer — a simpler, more chew-resistant mat is more practical until that phase passes.

## FAQ

**Does white noise actually help?** No dedicated peer-reviewed research on this specific question was located for puppies. It's a low-risk, commonly recommended practical tool for masking disruptive sound, not a claim this page treats as proven.

## Common Mistakes

- Sizing bedding to look cute rather than to fit the crate.
- Choosing a plush bed during a heavy teething phase.
- Expecting white noise alone to resolve a sleep problem without a consistent routine.

## Safety and Behavior Red Flags

Remove and replace any bedding your puppy has begun to shred or ingest pieces of; size bedding to the crate, not the other way around, to avoid reducing usable space below what [Puppy Crate Size Guide](/shop/crate-size-guide/) recommends.
