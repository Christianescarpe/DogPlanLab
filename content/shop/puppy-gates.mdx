<AnswerFirstSummary>
  Pressure-mounted gates are not rated for stairway-top use under ASTM F1004, the mandatory U.S. federal safety standard for gates and enclosures (16 C.F.R. Part 1239) — this standard specifically governs child safety gates, and the same mechanical failure risk (a pressure-fit gate dislodging under load) applies when a comparable product is used or marketed as a pet gate. Hardware-mounted gates are the only appropriate choice at the top of a staircase.
</AnswerFirstSummary>

## How to Choose

This is for households managing room-by-room access during house training, or wanting a lower-commitment alternative or complement to a playpen (see existing [Crate vs. Playpen](/shop/crate-vs-playpen/)) for open-plan spaces.

## What to Compare

Mounting type (pressure vs. hardware) matched correctly to location; height relative to your puppy's current and expected adult jumping ability; walk-through door vs. step-over only; width range or extendability for wide openings.

## Comparison

| Location | Correct type |
|---|---|
| Top of stairs | Hardware-mounted only — pressure-mounted gates are not rated for stair-top use |
| Doorway, no stairs | Either; pressure-mounted is simpler |
| Wide or open-plan opening | Extra-wide or configurable panel gate |
| Renting or no drilling | Pressure-mounted, away from stair tops |

## Best For / Not Ideal For

Pressure-mounted gates are not appropriate at the top of a staircase regardless of convenience — this is a genuine safety distinction, not a preference.

## FAQ

**Can a gate replace a crate or playpen?** Not for unsupervised confinement generally — see existing [Crate vs. Playpen](/shop/crate-vs-playpen/) for that decision. A gate manages space, not confinement in the same sense.

## Common Mistakes

- Using a pressure-mounted gate at the top of a staircase for convenience.
- Choosing a gate height without accounting for your puppy's expected adult jumping ability.
- Assuming any latch is puppy-proof without checking it yourself.

## Safety and Behavior Red Flags

Never substitute a pressure-mounted gate at the top of stairs; check for a latch a determined nose or paw can't accidentally release; verify the gap width is too narrow for your puppy's head to fit through.
