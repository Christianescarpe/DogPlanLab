## How to Choose

This is for anyone bathing a puppy per the existing bathing routine. Look for a dog-specific formulation (never human shampoo or soap), tear-free labeling for early baths near the face, fragrance-free or lightly-scented options for sensitive-skin puppies, and a puppy-specific — often gentler — formula vs. a general adult dog shampoo.

## Comparison

| Type | Best for |
|---|---|
| Tear-free puppy shampoo | First baths, general use |
| Fragrance-free or sensitive-skin formula | Puppies with known skin sensitivity |
| Medicated shampoo | Only under veterinary guidance for a diagnosed skin condition — not a general buying-guide recommendation |

## Best For / Not Ideal For

A medicated or heavily active-ingredient shampoo is not something to select on your own without veterinary guidance — that's a treatment decision, not a routine purchase.

## FAQ

**Can I use baby shampoo instead?** No — even "gentle" human formulations aren't matched to a dog's skin pH. Use a dog-specific product.

## Common Mistakes

- Reaching for human or baby shampoo in a pinch.
- Choosing a medicated shampoo without veterinary guidance.
- Using a non-tear-free formula for a young puppy's first baths.

## Safety and Behavior Red Flags

Keep shampoo out of eyes and ears during bathing. Discontinue and consult your veterinarian if you notice redness, itching, or irritation after using a new product, which could reflect a reaction rather than a technique problem.
