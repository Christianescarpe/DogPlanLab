<AnswerFirstSummary>
  Depends on the care type — nails roughly every couple of weeks, brushing depends on coat type, teeth benefit from daily brushing (several-times-weekly still effective), bathing is generally "as needed" rather than fixed.
</AnswerFirstSummary>

## By Care Type

| Care type | General guidance |
|---|---|
| Nail trims | Often every 2–4 weeks, adjusted to growth/wear |
| Brushing | Varies by coat type — occasional for short coats, several times a week or more for longer/double coats |
| Bathing | "As needed" rather than fixed — over-bathing can dry out skin |
| Teeth brushing | Daily is most effective; several times a week is realistic and still effective<sup>1</sup> |

*General starting points — your groomer or vet can tailor guidance to your puppy.*

## Why "As Needed" Beats a Rigid Schedule for Bathing

More frequent isn't automatically better — bathing can strip natural oils and disrupt the skin barrier.

## Common Mistakes

- Bathing on a fixed schedule regardless of need.
- Letting nails go too long.
- Assuming one frequency fits every coat type.
