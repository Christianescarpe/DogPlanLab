## Before the Appointment

Build comfort across paws, ears, mouth, brushing at home. Ask about a brief meet-and-greet visit. Choose a groomer with puppy experience specifically.

## What to Expect

First sessions are often intentionally short and light, not a full groom.

## After the Appointment

Keep pickup calm; a short positive activity afterward reinforces the whole experience was fine.

## Preparation Checklist

- [ ] Handling comfort built at home before the appointment
- [ ] Groomer confirmed to have puppy experience
- [ ] Meet-and-greet visit considered
- [ ] Realistic expectations — first visits often short and light

## Common Mistakes

- First-ever handling at the groomer with no home practice.
- Not confirming puppy experience.
- Expecting a full groom on visit one.

## Safety and Behavior Red Flags

Significant fear not improving across a couple of well-prepared visits — discuss with the groomer and consider a certified trainer/veterinary behaviorist.
