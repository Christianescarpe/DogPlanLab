## Basic Technique

1. One hand/arm under the chest, supporting the front.
2. Other hand/arm under the rear/hindquarters.
3. Lift smoothly, bringing your puppy close to your body.
4. Lower just as smoothly and predictably.

## Why Support Matters

Lifting only under the front legs doesn't distribute weight evenly and can feel insecure — which can itself contribute to struggling, separate from any purely behavioral cause.

## Building This Into Comfort Practice

Combine correct technique with the gradual comfort-building at [Puppy Biting When Picked Up](/puppy-biting/when-picked-up/) — they work together, not as substitutes.

## Do This / Not This

| Situation | Do This | Not This |
|---|---|---|
| Lifting | Support chest and rear together | Lift only under front legs |
| Holding | Bring close to your body | Hold away from your body |
| Setting down | Lower smoothly, predictably | Drop or set down abruptly |

## Common Mistakes

- Lifting under front legs alone.
- Grabbing the middle without rear support.
- Forcing a struggling puppy instead of pausing.

## Safety and Behavior Red Flags

Yelping or apparent pain specifically when picked up, even with correct technique — veterinary check.
