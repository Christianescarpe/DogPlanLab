  Usually light exposure, an established habit, or a genuine need — distinguishing which one applies changes the fix.

## Common Causes

Light exposure; an established habit (early waking reliably followed by attention/play/food); a genuine need (hunger, potty).

## What to Try

1. Darken the sleep space if light seems likely.
2. If habit-driven, keep the response deliberately boring.
3. Check feeding timing.
4. Be consistent across household members.

## Troubleshooting

| Pattern | Likely cause | What to try |
|---|---|---|
| Correlates with sunrise/light changes | Light exposure | Darken the sleep space |
| Reliably gets an exciting response | Established habit | Keep the response boring |
| Tied to hunger or potty need | Genuine need | Adjust feeding timing; treat as a need |

## Common Mistakes

- Starting an exciting day in response to early waking.
- Assuming habit when a need is the real driver.
- Inconsistent responses.

## Safety and Behavior Red Flags

Paired with other sleep/behavior changes, or not responding to consistent adjustment over a couple of weeks.
