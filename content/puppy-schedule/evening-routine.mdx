## A Sample Evening Routine (Adjust to Your Household)

**Example framework, not a required schedule.**

- 60–90 min before bed: last higher-energy activity.
- 30–60 min before bed: begin winding down — lower energy, dim lighting, calmer activities (see [Teaching Calm & Settle](/puppy-training/calm-settle/)).
- Just before bed: final calm potty trip, no play.
- Bedtime: straight to the sleep space, calmly.

## Why Gradual De-escalation Matters More Than Any Single Step

Full activity straight into "now settle" is harder than an hour of gradually lowering energy. The trend toward calm matters more than any specific activity.

## Connecting This to Specific Evening Problems

Reduces the *frequency* biting/zoomies/settling issues occur, while the in-the-moment response still lives on each symptom's own page.

## Common Mistakes

- High-energy play right up to bedtime.
- Expecting the routine alone to eliminate a symptom entirely.
- Inconsistency night to night.
